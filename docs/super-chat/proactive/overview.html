How Super Chat speaks first

Most assistants wait to be asked. This one notices when something in your work changes, does the first piece of work, and brings it to you at a good moment. You still decide everything.

● Plain rules, no AI, free● AI does work, costs money● You

Three promises

Quiet unless something happenedNo timer pinging you. A day with nothing new costs nothing and says nothing.
Brings the work, not a noticeNot "Dana emailed you", but "Dana asked which page goes live. Here's your reply, drafted."
Never acts for youIt reads and drafts. Sending, paying, deleting or sharing always waits for your yes.

What makes a strong signal

The rules compare each event with your current work. The closer the link, the stronger it is.

Strong
A reply in a thread, meeting or file you're already working on.Dana answers in the "Morocco landing pages" thread you started.
Medium
Someone you're working with, about something specific to that work.Dana, in a new mail, mentions "Morocco pages".
Weak
Two specific words from one piece of work, or mail from a person on it.A mail that mentions both "Polaris" and "overview deck".
Dropped
Bulk, automatic, or unrelated, or words that are everywhere in your mail.A weekly newsletter, a "no-reply" share notice, your own company's name.

Cleaner grouping, from day one
